Payment & Payout Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose

This Payment & Payout Policy explains the general rules that may apply to payments made by clients and payouts received by freelancers through WorkNest.

2. Payment Processing

Where payment services are available, WorkNest may use third-party payment providers to process transactions. Payment methods and availability may vary depending on the user's location and applicable provider requirements.

3. Payment Authorisation

Users must only use payment methods and financial accounts they are authorised to use. Users must provide accurate information required for payment processing and verification.

4. Project Payments

Clients may be required to pay for an agreed project according to the applicable WorkNest checkout, order or payment process. The specific payment terms may depend on the project and the services selected.

5. Freelancer Payouts

Subject to applicable conditions, freelancers may receive eligible earnings through a supported payout method. Payouts may be subject to verification, processing times, fees, disputes, refunds and payment-provider requirements.

6. Platform Fees

WorkNest may charge clients, freelancers or both platform or transaction fees. Any applicable fees should be displayed clearly before the relevant transaction is completed.

7. Currency

WorkNest may support multiple currencies. Currency conversion, exchange rates and related charges may be determined by the applicable payment provider or financial institution.

8. Payment Verification

WorkNest or its payment providers may require identity, business, payment or other verification information before certain transactions or payouts can be completed.

9. Payout Holds and Reviews

A payment or payout may be temporarily delayed or placed under review where necessary to investigate fraud, security issues, disputes, chargebacks, verification requirements or compliance obligations.

10. Refunds

Refunds are handled according to the applicable WorkNest Refund & Cancellation Policy and the rules of the relevant payment provider.

11. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Users must not submit fraudulent or unauthorised chargebacks. Payment disputes may be reviewed using available transaction information and applicable provider procedures.

12. Fraud Prevention

WorkNest may use reasonable measures to detect and prevent suspicious transactions, payment fraud, account abuse and other financial risks.

13. Taxes

Users are responsible for understanding and meeting their own tax obligations arising from payments or earnings received through WorkNest, except where applicable law requires WorkNest or a payment provider to take specific actions.

14. Payment Provider Terms

Third-party payment providers may have their own terms, privacy policies, verification procedures, fees and transaction rules. Users may be required to comply with those terms when using a supported payment method.

15. Unauthorised Transactions

Users should promptly report suspected unauthorised transactions or compromised payment information to WorkNest and, where appropriate, to the relevant payment provider or financial institution.

16. Payout Information

Freelancers are responsible for providing accurate payout information. WorkNest is not responsible for delays or failed transfers caused by incorrect information supplied by the user, subject to applicable law.

17. Changes to Payment Services

WorkNest may add, remove or modify supported payment methods, payout methods, fees or payment features from time to time.

18. Changes to This Policy

WorkNest may update this Policy when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this page with an updated date.

19. Contact
