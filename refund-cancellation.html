Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the general rules that may apply when a client or freelancer cancels a project, requests a refund or experiences a payment-related dispute on WorkNest.

2. Project Agreements

Clients and freelancers should agree clearly on the project scope, deliverables, price and deadline before work begins. These details may be considered when a cancellation or refund request is reviewed.

3. Client Cancellation

A client may request cancellation of a project. Whether a refund is available may depend on the stage of the project, work already completed, agreed terms, applicable WorkNest rules and applicable law.

4. Freelancer Cancellation

Freelancers should not accept projects they cannot reasonably complete. If a freelancer cancels or fails to deliver agreed work, the client may request a review, cancellation or refund in accordance with the applicable process.

5. Completed Work

A refund may not be appropriate where the agreed services have already been properly completed and delivered. Any dispute about the quality or completeness of work may be reviewed under the WorkNest dispute-resolution process.

6. Refund Requests

Refund requests should be submitted through the designated WorkNest process where available. Users may be asked to provide relevant project information, communications or evidence.

7. Disputed Transactions

Where a project or payment is disputed, WorkNest may review available information from the client, freelancer and relevant payment provider before determining what platform action is appropriate under applicable policies and law.

8. Payment Provider Rules

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. Those providers may have separate terms, processing rules and dispute procedures. WorkNest cannot override the rules of an independent payment provider.

9. Fraudulent Refund Requests

Users must not make dishonest refund claims, provide false evidence, manipulate transactions or abuse cancellation procedures. Suspected fraud may result in account restrictions or other action.

10. Platform Fees

Where WorkNest charges service or transaction fees, the treatment of those fees following a cancellation or refund will be explained in the applicable pricing or payment terms.

11. Chargebacks

Users should contact WorkNest regarding transaction disputes where the platform provides a dispute process. Unauthorised or fraudulent chargebacks may be investigated and may result in account restrictions where permitted by applicable law.

12. Digital and Other Services

For digital services, downloads or work that has already been supplied, refund rights may depend on the nature of the service, the user's location, applicable consumer law and the specific agreement between the parties.

13. Legal Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Policy is intended to remove or restrict mandatory consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

14. Timing of Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the time required for the funds to reach the user's payment method may depend on the payment provider and financial institution involved.

15. Exceptions

WorkNest may consider exceptional circumstances, including technical problems, fraud, unauthorised transactions or other circumstances requiring additional review.

16. Changes to This Policy

WorkNest may update this Policy when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this page with an updated date.
