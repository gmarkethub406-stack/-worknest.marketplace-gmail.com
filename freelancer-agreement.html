Freelancer Agreement

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose

This Freelancer Agreement sets out the general terms that apply when a freelancer uses WorkNest to offer professional services, communicate with clients or complete projects.

2. Independent Freelancer

A freelancer using WorkNest is an independent service provider and is not an employee, partner, agent or representative of WorkNest unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

3. Accurate Information

Freelancers must provide accurate and current information about their identity, skills, qualifications, experience, services, portfolio and professional background. Freelancers must not falsely claim qualifications or experience they do not possess.

4. Services and Proposals

Freelancers are responsible for reviewing project requirements before accepting work. Any proposal, quotation or offer should accurately describe the services the freelancer intends to provide, together with any agreed price, timeframe and deliverables.

5. Accepted Projects

Once a freelancer accepts a project, the freelancer should make reasonable efforts to complete the agreed work according to the project requirements and agreed deadline.

6. Communication

Freelancers should communicate professionally with clients and use WorkNest communication tools where provided. Fraudulent, abusive, threatening, discriminatory or unlawful communications are prohibited.

7. Quality and Professional Conduct

Freelancers are expected to provide services professionally and honestly. A freelancer must not knowingly submit stolen, fraudulent, plagiarised or unlawfully obtained work.

8. Intellectual Property

Ownership and permitted use of work created for a client should be determined by the agreement between the freelancer and client, subject to applicable law and any WorkNest policies. Freelancers must have the necessary rights to materials they provide.

9. Confidentiality

Freelancers should protect confidential information received from clients and must not disclose or misuse confidential information except where authorised or required by law.

10. Payments and Payouts

Where payment and payout services are available, transactions may be processed through approved third-party payment providers. Freelancer payouts may be subject to applicable fees, verification requirements, payment-provider rules, refunds, disputes and other applicable WorkNest policies.

11. Taxes and Legal Responsibilities

Freelancers are responsible for understanding and meeting their own tax, registration, licensing and other legal obligations arising from their freelance activities, except where applicable law requires WorkNest or a payment provider to take specific actions.

12. Prohibited Conduct

Freelancers must not use WorkNest for fraud, scams, money laundering, illegal services, account manipulation, impersonation, harassment, intellectual-property infringement, malware or other prohibited activity.

13. Off-Platform Transactions

Freelancers must follow WorkNest rules concerning payments and communications outside the platform. Users must not attempt to bypass applicable WorkNest fees, safety systems or transaction protections where such conduct is prohibited by platform rules.

14. Reviews and Ratings

Freelancers must not manipulate reviews or ratings, purchase fraudulent reviews, threaten clients over reviews or otherwise interfere with the integrity of WorkNest's reputation systems.

15. Account Security

Freelancers are responsible for keeping their account credentials secure and must promptly report suspected unauthorised access or suspicious activity.

16. Suspension or Termination

WorkNest may restrict, suspend or terminate a freelancer's account where there is a reasonable basis to believe that the freelancer has violated this Agreement, WorkNest policies, applicable law or platform safety requirements.

17. Disputes

Freelancers should use the WorkNest dispute-resolution process where available. Project disputes may also be subject to the terms agreed between the freelancer and client and applicable law.

18. Changes to This Agreement

WorkNest may update this Agreement when necessary. Updated versions will be published on the platform with an updated effective date.
