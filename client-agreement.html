Client Agreement

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose

This Client Agreement sets out the general terms that apply when a client uses WorkNest to find freelancers, publish projects, communicate with service providers or purchase professional services.

2. Client Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for providing accurate project information, requirements, budgets, deadlines and other information necessary for freelancers to understand the work.

3. Job Listings

Clients must publish legitimate projects and must not create misleading, fraudulent or unlawful job listings. Project descriptions should accurately explain the services being requested.

4. Selecting Freelancers

Clients are responsible for reviewing freelancer profiles, proposals, qualifications and other available information before selecting a freelancer. WorkNest may provide marketplace tools but does not guarantee the quality, availability or suitability of any particular freelancer.

5. Project Agreements

Before work begins, clients and freelancers should clearly understand the agreed services, deliverables, price, deadline and any important project conditions.

6. Communication

Clients should communicate professionally with freelancers and use WorkNest communication tools where provided. Harassment, threats, fraud, abusive conduct and unlawful communications are prohibited.

7. Payments

Where payment services are available, clients may be required to pay through approved payment methods or third-party payment providers. Applicable fees, payment requirements, refunds, cancellations and disputes are governed by the relevant WorkNest policies and payment-provider terms.

8. Authorisation and Payment Security

Clients must only use payment methods they are authorised to use. Clients must not attempt to use stolen payment information, fraudulent payment methods or other unauthorised financial instruments.

9. Project Changes

If project requirements, deliverables, price or deadlines change after work begins, the client and freelancer should agree to the changes clearly before proceeding where reasonably possible.

10. Intellectual Property

Ownership and permitted use of completed work should be determined by the agreement between the client and freelancer, subject to applicable law and any WorkNest policies. Clients must not request or knowingly accept materials that infringe another person's intellectual-property rights.

11. Confidential Information

Clients should protect confidential information received from freelancers and must not misuse or disclose confidential information except where authorised or required by law.

12. Reviews and Ratings

Clients may provide honest feedback about completed projects. Clients must not submit fraudulent reviews, manipulate ratings, threaten freelancers over reviews or attempt to artificially influence WorkNest reputation systems.

13. Prohibited Activities

Clients must not use WorkNest for fraud, scams, money laundering, illegal services, harassment, impersonation, intellectual-property infringement, malware or other activity prohibited by WorkNest rules or applicable law.

14. Off-Platform Transactions

Clients must follow WorkNest rules concerning payments and communications outside the platform. Clients must not attempt to bypass applicable WorkNest fees, safety systems or transaction protections where such conduct is prohibited.

15. Disputes and Cancellations

Clients should first use the WorkNest dispute-resolution and cancellation procedures where available. Refunds or other remedies may depend on the circumstances of the project, applicable WorkNest policies, payment-provider rules and applicable law.

16. Account Security

Clients are responsible for protecting their account credentials and should promptly report suspected unauthorised access or suspicious activity.

17. Account Suspension or Termination

WorkNest may restrict, suspend or terminate a client's account where there is a reasonable basis to believe that the client has violated this Agreement, WorkNest policies, applicable law or platform safety requirements.

18. Taxes and Legal Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for understanding and meeting their own tax, business, licensing and other legal obligations associated with projects they post or services they purchase, except where applicable law requires WorkNest or a payment provider to take specific actions.

19. Changes to This Agreement

WorkNest may update this Agreement when necessary. Updated versions will be published on the platform with an updated effective date.
