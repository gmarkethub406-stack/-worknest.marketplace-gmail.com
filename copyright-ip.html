Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy explains how WorkNest handles intellectual property rights, copyrighted material, user content, project work and intellectual property complaints on the platform.

1. Purpose

WorkNest respects the intellectual property rights of creators, freelancers, clients, businesses and other rights holders. Users must only upload, publish, share or use content that they have the legal right to use.

2. User-Owned Content

Subject to the agreements between clients and freelancers, users generally retain ownership of content, materials, profiles, portfolios and other intellectual property they create or submit to WorkNest.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their content does not infringe another person's copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity or other legal rights.

3. Project Work

Ownership and permitted use of work created for a client should be determined by the agreement between the client and freelancer.

Where appropriate, the parties should clearly agree on matters such as ownership, licensing, usage rights, source files, commercial use, attribution, modifications and portfolio rights before work begins.

4. WorkNest Platform Content

WorkNest's branding, logos, website design, software, platform features, text, graphics and other platform materials may be protected by intellectual property laws.

Users may not copy, reproduce, modify, distribute, sell or create derivative works from WorkNest platform materials without appropriate authorization.

5. Copyright Infringement

Users must not upload, publish, sell, distribute or otherwise use copyrighted material without permission or another valid legal basis.

Examples may include unauthorized copies of books, photographs, videos, software, music, designs, articles, artwork, templates or other protected works.

6. Trademarks

Users must not misuse another person's or company's trademarks, logos, trade names or branding in a way that creates unlawful confusion or falsely suggests sponsorship, affiliation or endorsement.

7. Reporting Intellectual Property Violations

If you believe content available through WorkNest infringes your copyright or other intellectual property rights, you may contact WorkNest with relevant information so the matter can be reviewed.

A report should generally include:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Identification of the copyrighted or protected work.
  • A description of the material you believe is infringing.
  • The location or link to the material on WorkNest.
  • An explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorized.
  • Any supporting evidence reasonably available to you.

8. Review of Reports

WorkNest may review intellectual property complaints and may take reasonable action where appropriate, including removing or restricting access to reported content, contacting relevant users, requesting additional information or suspending accounts where required by the circumstances.

WorkNest does not guarantee that every complaint will result in removal or that every reported claim will be accepted.

9. Counter-Notices and Disputes

If content is restricted or removed because of an intellectual property complaint, the affected user may contact WorkNest with relevant information explaining why the content should remain available.

WorkNest may request additional evidence before making a decision. Nothing in this policy removes any legal rights or remedies available to users under applicable law.

10. Repeat Infringement

WorkNest may take stronger enforcement action against accounts that repeatedly infringe intellectual property rights or repeatedly submit credible violations of this policy.

11. False or Abusive Reports

Users must not knowingly submit false, misleading or abusive intellectual property complaints.

Deliberate misuse of the reporting process may result in account restrictions or other appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Content

WorkNest may contain links, content or services provided by third parties. Users are responsible for respecting the intellectual property rights associated with third-party materials.

13. User Responsibility

Each user is responsible for obtaining any permissions, licences, releases or other rights required for the content and services they provide through WorkNest.

14. Cooperation With Legal Requests

WorkNest may cooperate with lawful requests from courts, regulators, law enforcement authorities or other legally authorized bodies concerning intellectual property disputes or unlawful activity.

15. Changes to This Policy

WorkNest may update this policy when necessary to reflect changes to the platform, applicable law or business practices. Updated versions will be published on the platform with a revised update date.
