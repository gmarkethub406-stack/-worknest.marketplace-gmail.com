Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WorkNest may collect, use, store and protect information when users access or use the WorkNest marketplace.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use WorkNest, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account type, profile information, skills, professional information, job listings, messages, transaction information and other information you voluntarily provide.

3. Account Information

When you create an account, WorkNest may use your information to create and maintain your account, provide marketplace services, communicate with you and help protect the platform.

4. Profile and Marketplace Information

Information that you choose to publish as part of a freelancer profile, job listing, portfolio or other marketplace feature may be visible to other users or visitors where the relevant feature is publicly accessible.

5. Messages and Communications

WorkNest may process messages and other communications sent through the platform in order to provide communication features, protect users, investigate reports and maintain platform security, subject to applicable law.

6. Payment Information

Where payment services are available, payments may be handled by third-party payment providers. WorkNest may receive limited transaction information necessary to provide marketplace services, while sensitive payment details may be handled directly by the relevant payment provider.

7. How We Use Information

WorkNest may use information to provide and improve services, manage accounts, facilitate projects, process transactions, communicate with users, prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes, maintain security and comply with legal obligations.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

WorkNest may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support essential website functions, remember preferences, improve performance and understand how the platform is used. Additional information may be provided in the WorkNest Cookie Policy.

9. Service Providers

WorkNest may use trusted third-party providers for services such as hosting, authentication, email delivery, analytics, payments, security and other technical functions. Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and as permitted by applicable law.

10. Data Security

WorkNest will take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

WorkNest may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations and protect legitimate business interests.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and rights to object to certain processing. These rights are subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

13. International Data Transfers

Because WorkNest is intended to operate internationally, information may be processed in countries other than the country where a user lives. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

14. Children's Privacy

WorkNest is intended for users who are legally permitted to use online freelance marketplace services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

15. Third-Party Links

WorkNest may contain links to third-party websites or services. WorkNest is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third parties. Users should review the privacy policies of those services before providing information.

16. Changes to This Policy

WorkNest may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Changes will be published on this page together with an updated date.
