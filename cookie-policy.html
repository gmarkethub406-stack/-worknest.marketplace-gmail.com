Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information, maintain sessions and provide certain features.

WorkNest may use analytics technologies to understand how users interact with the platform, identify technical problems and improve website performance. Any analytics service used will be subject to its applicable privacy terms and WorkNest's privacy requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

Certain third-party services used by WorkNest may place or access cookies or similar technologies. These services may include hosting, authentication, analytics, security, communication or payment providers.

7. Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow users to control or delete cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some WorkNest features.

8. Consent

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, WorkNest will seek consent through an appropriate notice or preference mechanism.
