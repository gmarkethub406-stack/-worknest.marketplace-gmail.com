Community & Conduct Rules

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our Community Standard

WorkNest is designed to be a professional marketplace where clients and freelancers can work together respectfully, safely and honestly.

2. Treat Others With Respect

Users should communicate respectfully and professionally. Harassment, bullying, threats, intimidation and abusive behaviour are not permitted.

3. Be Honest

Users must provide truthful information about their identity, skills, experience, services, projects and transactions. Impersonation and deliberate deception are prohibited.

4. Professional Communication

Messages should remain relevant to legitimate work or marketplace activity. Users should avoid spam, excessive unsolicited messages and inappropriate communications.

5. Protect Personal Information

Users should avoid publicly sharing sensitive personal information belonging to themselves or another person. Personal information obtained through WorkNest must not be misused or disclosed without appropriate authorisation.

6. Safe Transactions

Users should use official WorkNest transaction and payment processes where available. Suspicious payment requests, fraudulent transactions and attempts to bypass platform protections may be investigated.

7. Genuine Reviews

Reviews and ratings must represent genuine experiences. Fake reviews, rating manipulation, review extortion and other attempts to manipulate reputation systems are prohibited.

8. Intellectual Property

Users must respect copyrights, trademarks, patents and other intellectual-property rights. Content should only be uploaded, shared or sold where the user has the necessary rights.

9. Prohibited Content

Users must not post or distribute unlawful, fraudulent, malicious, threatening, abusive or otherwise prohibited content through WorkNest.

10. No Fraud or Scams

WorkNest must not be used for scams, identity theft, fraudulent job listings, fraudulent services, money laundering or other dishonest activity.

11. No Harmful Software

Users must not distribute viruses, ransomware, malware, malicious scripts or other software intended to damage, compromise or gain unauthorised access to systems.

12. Protect the Marketplace

Users must not interfere with WorkNest systems, attempt to bypass security controls, access accounts without permission or intentionally disrupt platform services.

13. Reporting Problems

Users are encouraged to report suspected scams, harassment, security problems, fraudulent listings or other serious violations to WorkNest.

14. Enforcement

WorkNest may investigate reports and take reasonable action, including removing content, restricting features, suspending accounts or terminating accounts where appropriate and permitted by applicable law.

15. False Reports

Reports should be made honestly and in good faith. Users must not knowingly submit false reports to harass, punish or interfere with another user.

16. Changes to These Rules

WorkNest may update these Community & Conduct Rules when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this page with an updated date.

17. Contact

Community and safety concerns may be sent to:

worknestmarketplace@gmail.com

Important: These are general platform rules and should be reviewed before commercial launch to ensure they align with applicable UK law, online platform requirements, privacy rules, equality requirements and WorkNest's final moderation process.
