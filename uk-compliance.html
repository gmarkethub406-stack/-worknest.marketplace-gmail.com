UK Compliance Checklist

Last updated: September 2026

This checklist identifies important areas WorkNest should review before operating commercially as an online freelance marketplace serving users in the United Kingdom.

It is a practical planning checklist and does not constitute legal, tax, financial or regulatory advice.

1. Business Structure

  • Choose and document the legal structure of the business.
  • Register the business where required.
  • Maintain accurate company and ownership records.
  • Confirm the business's operating and registered address requirements.
  • Obtain appropriate professional and business insurance where necessary.

2. Terms and Platform Rules

  • Publish clear Terms of Service.
  • Publish Client and Freelancer agreements where appropriate.
  • Publish Acceptable Use and Community Rules.
  • Publish Refund and Cancellation rules.
  • Publish Payment and Payout rules.
  • Publish Dispute Resolution procedures.
  • Publish Copyright and Intellectual Property rules.
  • Publish Safety and Fraud rules.

3. Privacy and Data Protection

  • Determine the business's role as controller or processor for relevant personal data.
  • Maintain a clear Privacy Policy.
  • Identify the personal information collected from users.
  • Document the lawful bases relied upon for processing personal data.
  • Provide appropriate information about data retention.
  • Provide appropriate processes for handling data-subject rights.
  • Review international data transfers where personal data leaves the UK.
  • Use appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
  • Maintain appropriate records of processing where required.
  • Assess whether a Data Protection Impact Assessment is required for particular processing.
  • Review whether an independent Data Protection Officer is legally required.

4. Cookies and Online Tracking

  • Identify cookies and similar technologies used by WorkNest.
  • Classify technologies according to their purpose.
  • Provide appropriate information about cookies.
  • Obtain consent where required before placing or using non-essential technologies.
  • Provide appropriate controls for withdrawing consent.

5. Consumer Protection

  • Review whether WorkNest is acting as a trader, marketplace operator or intermediary.
  • Clearly explain the nature of WorkNest's services.
  • Provide transparent information about prices, fees and charges.
  • Explain cancellation and refund arrangements clearly.
  • Review consumer rights that may apply to digital services and online contracts.
  • Ensure important contractual information is presented clearly before users commit to a transaction.

6. Marketplace Responsibilities

  • Clearly explain the role WorkNest plays between clients and freelancers.
  • Explain whether WorkNest is a contracting party or intermediary for particular transactions.
  • Provide appropriate information about professional users where legally required.
  • Maintain reasonable procedures for reporting illegal or prohibited activity.
  • Review applicable UK online-platform and marketplace obligations.

7. Payments

  • Use a reputable payment service provider appropriate for the business model.
  • Confirm whether any payment or money-handling activity requires authorization or registration.
  • Do not hold or transmit customer funds in a way that creates unauthorized regulated activity.
  • Complete appropriate identity and business verification requirements.
  • Maintain accurate transaction records.
  • Define refund, cancellation and chargeback procedures.
  • Protect payment information and avoid storing sensitive card information unnecessarily.

8. Fraud and Financial Crime

  • Maintain reasonable fraud-prevention procedures.
  • Monitor unusual transactions where appropriate.
  • Provide procedures for reporting suspicious activity.
  • Review applicable anti-money-laundering requirements.
  • Review sanctions and prohibited-party screening requirements where relevant.
  • Keep appropriate records of fraud and security incidents.

9. Intellectual Property

  • Maintain a Copyright and Intellectual Property Policy.
  • Provide a process for reporting alleged infringement.
  • Review procedures for responding to credible infringement complaints.
  • Ensure WorkNest's own branding and software rights are appropriately protected.
  • Clarify ownership and licensing of client and freelancer project work.

10. Employment and Freelancer Classification

  • Clearly explain that freelancers are responsible for their own independent business activities where applicable.
  • Review whether any relationship created through WorkNest could legally constitute employment or worker status.
  • Review relevant UK employment and tax considerations.
  • Avoid contractual language that inaccurately describes the legal relationship.

11. Tax

  • Obtain appropriate professional tax advice for the business structure.
  • Review VAT registration and charging requirements.
  • Maintain appropriate business and transaction records.
  • Review tax obligations associated with marketplace fees and commissions.
  • Consider tax reporting obligations that may apply to digital platform operators.
  • Make clear that freelancers may have their own tax obligations.

12. Security

  • Use HTTPS for the production website.
  • Use secure authentication and password handling.
  • Protect administrator accounts with strong authentication.
  • Use appropriate access controls.
  • Keep software and dependencies updated.
  • Maintain backups and appropriate recovery procedures.
  • Monitor security incidents.
  • Maintain a process for responding to personal-data breaches.

13. Communications

  • Provide accurate contact information.
  • Explain how users can contact WorkNest for support.
  • Review rules governing marketing emails and electronic communications.
  • Provide appropriate consent and unsubscribe mechanisms where required.
  • Separate transactional notifications from promotional communications where appropriate.

14. Accessibility

  • Design the platform to be usable by people with different accessibility needs.
  • Use readable text and appropriate colour contrast.
  • Provide meaningful labels for forms and controls.
  • Test navigation and important user journeys for accessibility.
  • Review applicable UK accessibility requirements for the services offered.

15. Complaints

  • Create a clear customer complaint process.
  • Record material complaints and responses.
  • Provide reasonable communication about complaint outcomes.
  • Review escalation procedures for serious or unresolved complaints.

16. Disputes

  • Maintain a clear dispute-resolution procedure.
  • Explain evidence and review procedures.
  • Explain applicable refund and cancellation processes.
  • Make clear that mandatory legal rights are not removed by platform rules.

17. Platform Monitoring and Moderation

  • Maintain procedures for detecting prohibited activity.
  • Provide reporting mechanisms for users.
  • Document account suspension and termination procedures.
  • Maintain appropriate records of serious safety or fraud incidents.
  • Review applicable UK requirements for online platforms and illegal-content reporting or moderation.

18. Third-Party Providers

  • Maintain a list of important service providers.
  • Review data-processing arrangements with relevant providers.
  • Review payment-provider responsibilities.
  • Review email, hosting, analytics and authentication providers.
  • Confirm that third-party services are suitable for the intended UK user base.

19. Records and Governance

  • Keep accurate business records.
  • Document important security and compliance decisions.
  • Maintain appropriate contracts with suppliers and service providers.
  • Review policies periodically.
  • Document significant changes to the platform.

20. Before Commercial Launch

  • Complete legal review of the Terms of Service.
  • Complete UK data-protection review.
  • Complete consumer-law review.
  • Complete payment and financial-regulation review.
  • Complete tax review.
  • Complete security review.
  • Confirm business registration requirements.
  • Confirm domain ownership.
  • Configure production email.
  • Configure real authentication and database services.
  • Configure production payment services.
  • Test account creation, login, messaging, jobs, payments and disputes.
  • Test mobile and desktop versions.
  • Test security and error handling.
  • Publish the final approved legal documents.

21. Important Legal Review

This checklist is not a statement that WorkNest is legally compliant simply because the listed items have been completed.

UK requirements can depend on the business structure, services, users, payment arrangements, data processing, geographic scope and the precise way the marketplace operates.

Before commercial launch, WorkNest should obtain advice from appropriately qualified UK legal, tax, privacy and financial professionals to determine which requirements apply.
