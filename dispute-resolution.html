Dispute Resolution Procedure

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose

This procedure explains how WorkNest may handle disputes between clients and freelancers relating to projects, payments, deliverables or other marketplace transactions.

2. First Step: Direct Resolution

Where appropriate, the client and freelancer should first try to resolve the issue directly through WorkNest communication tools. Users should communicate respectfully and explain the issue clearly.

3. Opening a Dispute

If the parties cannot resolve the issue, an eligible user may submit a dispute through the designated WorkNest process, where available.

4. Information and Evidence

WorkNest may request relevant information such as the project description, agreed deliverables, messages, files, payment records, deadlines and other evidence reasonably necessary to review the dispute.

5. Review Process

WorkNest may review information supplied by the parties and relevant transaction records. The review process may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the dispute.

6. Temporary Measures

Where reasonably necessary, WorkNest may temporarily restrict transactions, payments, account features or other platform activity while a dispute or security concern is being reviewed.

7. Possible Outcomes

Depending on the circumstances, WorkNest may close the dispute, facilitate an agreed resolution, approve or deny an eligible refund, adjust a transaction where permitted, or take other platform action consistent with applicable policies and law.

8. Payment Provider Disputes

Some payment disputes may also be subject to the rules and procedures of the relevant payment provider, bank or financial institution.

9. Fraud and Abuse

WorkNest may investigate suspected fraud, false evidence, payment abuse, account manipulation or other misuse connected with a dispute.

10. Good-Faith Participation

Users must provide accurate information and participate in the dispute process honestly. Knowingly submitting false evidence or using disputes to harass another user may result in account restrictions.

11. Time Limits

WorkNest may establish time limits for opening disputes, submitting evidence or responding to requests. Any applicable time limits will be communicated through the relevant process.

12. No Guarantee of Outcome

Submitting a dispute does not guarantee a refund, payment reversal or other particular outcome. Decisions may depend on the available evidence, applicable WorkNest policies and applicable law.

13. Legal Rights

Nothing in this procedure is intended to remove or restrict rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

14. Contact

15. Changes to This Procedure

WorkNest may update this procedure when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this page with an updated date.
